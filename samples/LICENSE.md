# Example provenance

The source illustrations, graph/chart facts, questions, teaching text, twenty process evaluation diagrams and five additional chart evaluation diagrams were created for TouchMap on 3 October 2026. They are supplied under CC BY 4.0, attribution **TouchMap contributors**. They contain no personal information and use an invented workshop for unfamiliar-relationship testing. All chart values are explicitly authored teaching examples, not observations of weather, energy use or people.

The WAV recordings were generated from that owned text with ElevenLabs `eleven_flash_v2_5`, premade Bella voice `hpp4J3VqNfWAUOO0d1Us`, on an active paid account. No voice was cloned. Generated recordings are supplied with this educational example under the provider's applicable output-use terms, distinct from the source-illustration licence. The provider/voice and text/file hashes are recorded per asset. No proprietary voice model is distributed.

`scripts/create-samples.py` reproduces source geometry and packages. Existing audio is reused. Generating replacement audio requires a configured authorised voice, provider key and the explicit `--cloud-consent` option. Critical pronunciation and accessibility should be reviewed with target users; hash and duration checks alone do not establish pronunciation quality.

An author declaration in a package is preserved as supplied and must be accepted by a receiver. It is not an independent certification of accessibility, factual truth or author identity.
